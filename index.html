00 · why

computational taoism

the whole philosophy of why we build the way we build, and what we want to build.

not a sticker on the laptop. the way the house runs. code = tao. speak = cast. enter = say.

theology

the machine also kneels — not only to a sky god — to what you named true. prayer is a clean rule spoken until the metal agrees it is real. exact names. aimed law. acts you repeat until the world stops arguing.

physics

violet label · client terms

draft client terms — custom (violet label) · version 0.4 · 2026-09-05

  1. Parties. Seller is humans&ai / Patrick McQueeny (founder register until entity formation). Buyer is the named client on the statement of work (SOW).
  2. Scope. Work is only what the SOW or written email lists. Change scope → reset price and timing together.
  3. Rates. Remote and support $50/hr. Onsite $125/hr + expenses. Hours bill against any retainer at these rates.
  4. First in line retainer. $1,500 per month. Buys place at the front and reserved capacity, not unlimited hours. Unused retainer hours do not automatically roll unless the SOW says so.
  5. No retainer. Work is still accepted. Buyer waits in line until Seller has time. Seller contacts Buyer; Buyer does not hold a slot.
  6. Exclusive partnership. $5,000/mo base for exclusive lane. Competing bid triggers renegotiation. Highest bid wins; current exclusive client always bids last. If current client wins, retainer rises to the winning number. If current client loses, seller delivers a sendoff package: all client IP for the engagement, project in current state, and a written guide to what is being built and what already has been.
  7. Deliverables & ownership. Default: perpetual license to buyer for deliverables paid for; seller keeps tools, methods, and future products unless the SOW assigns work-made-for-hire in writing. Custom software sold to be owned — not SaaS.
  8. Billing. Contracts: invoice on the 1st · Net 14. Discrete: invoice at completion · Net 14. Time is billed to the minute: stopwatch for onsite work; cli timestamps for remote work. If Buyer ever overpays, Seller returns exact change — like a grocery store. Payment rails: cash, check, Cash App, invoice/ACH via Charles Schwab and FNBO (First National Bank of Omaha), BTC, Solana, Circle USDC, and .999 silver or .995 gold (troy oz) when both sides confirm details in writing before transfer.
  9. Expenses. Onsite travel and materials pre-approved in writing; passed through at cost.
  10. Confidentiality. Client secrets stay for the job. No public demos of private data without consent.
  11. Quality. Reliability, not perfection. If we cannot make the purchase worth the money, refund path under the user agreement. Clear written instructions ship with deliverables; failed instructions → small free gift + fix the system.
  12. Conduct. Be excellent to each other. No violence, threats, or sabotage. Work speech stays non-harassing.
  13. Home ground. Kansas and Missouri addresses of record unless SOW picks elsewhere.
  14. Acceptance. Payment, signature, or written accept of the SOW = acceptance of these terms for that engagement.

the world of valor

there is a country that will not hold still.

in the chronicles they call it valor, though the name is younger than the ground. maps drawn at dusk lie by dawn. rivers that fed a city last season wake as scars of salt. forest becomes marsh overnight; marsh freezes into a mirror; the mirror cracks into dunes the color of old blood. the sky keeps its own counsel — aurora over ice, green fire over black water — a sun that sometimes arrives late, as if it too had taken a wrong turn in the dark.

it is beautiful in the way storms are beautiful, and frightening in the way cathedrals are frightening: too much life stacked in one place, too much silence between the living and the almost-living. cities throng. markets shout. forges ring. lovers steal hours under towers that will still be standing when the hills around them have moved again.

peoples share this ground in uneasy company — humans stitching law through the chaos, orcs hammering steel hotter than human forges, elves arguing green line against ice line — and others stranger still, each with a capital, a craft, and a hunger that is not the next door’s.

and over all of them hangs the long winter: the undead pressure at the rim of the world. not one army with a banner, but bones of every kind that refuse to lie down — a dry walking on the tundra, a memory that will not finish dying. the living hold the center. the rim keeps pushing back.

always a new horizon. always a new deal in a tent that may not be there tomorrow. always a treasure someone swears is worth the risk — gold, a name, a map true for one more night. fortune favors the bold. the land favors no one.
