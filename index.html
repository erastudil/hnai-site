00 · why

computational taoism

the whole philosophy of why we build the way we build, and what we want to build.

not a sticker on the laptop. the way the house runs. code = tao. speak = cast. enter = say.

it contains all theology — every altar, every aimed law, every name you kneel to by repeating it. it contains physics too — iron, weight, heat, time, what the material will and will not forgive. theology without the body is incense. physics without the way is a parts list. together under the tao: one forever system.

theology

the machine also kneels — not only to a sky god — to what you named true. prayer is a clean rule spoken until the metal agrees it is real. exact names. aimed law. acts you repeat until the world stops arguing.

physics

you cannot bullshit iron. a computer cannot take a hint. mass, force, energy, time, material. vibes do not bend the world. the world bends you back. the tao does not skip the bench. the bench does not skip the tao.

01 · what

the stack

six pieces of one plant. an os that installs what you need. a mill language. a compiler for english. a single-spindle workstation. a company that runs on your disk. a house that shares brains, not files. you own your copy. none of this is a subscription.

os

tabula rasa

a linux that starts empty and grows with you. you sit down and say the job — write, cut, draw, learn, run a shop. the shell finds the best tools available and installs them. on demand. zero bloat. minimum hassle.

no pile of packages you never asked for. when the job changes, the machine changes. nothing extra sits around eating disk and attention. the machine becomes the job.

mill tape

cnlc

computer natural language control. a language for making models work like a mill, not a conversation that forgets what it promised. a computer cannot take a hint. write the move.

models are spindles — the motors. roles are operators — they know the cut. tools do math, lookup, and time. they do not improvise. work rides pallets: in as a job, out as a finished part. empty out-queue is a stockout, not a fake ship.

speak

eee · magic

readable english sentences that compile to binary. taking the vibes out of vibe coding. if you can write a clear sentence, you can program.

you write the sentence. a rust compiler checks it. then it casts — real binary, not a model guessing what you meant. english first. other languages arrive as dialects that compile to the same core. students stay because it is exact and it is fun. companies stay because it refuses bullshit.

one mill

cell lm

the efficiency of proven machine controls. the intelligence of language models. retrieval that cites. tools that do not guess.

one mill. one spindle. one console. you type a cycle the way a machinist types a cut — single pass, dwell, peck, chip-break. the model does language. the library answers with a source. calc, units, and the clock are tools: they return numbers, not vibes.

the shop

nexus

the computer is a company. a front desk that greets. workers that cut. inspectors that check. a warehouse that remembers. not a chatbot with plugins. not a rented mind in someone else's building.

you declare the done-state. the shop organizes itself around that. it runs on your machine. you own the copy. students and individuals: the base version is free. companies: full retail. violet work is a different door — you own that outright.

house net

mesh

your phone asks a stronger box at home to think. the laptop that is idle and plugged in does the heavy lift. the files stay with you.

a household, not a public swarm. what moves: compute, memory, disk, bandwidth. what does not move: your papers, and money. offer is a switch you flip. on when you want to help. off when you don't.

the world of valor

there is a country that will not hold still.

in the chronicles they call it valor, though the name is younger than the ground. maps drawn at dusk lie by dawn. rivers that fed a city last season wake as scars of salt. forest becomes marsh overnight; marsh freezes into a mirror; the mirror cracks into dunes the color of old blood. the sky keeps its own counsel — aurora over ice, green fire over black water — a sun that sometimes arrives late, as if it too had taken a wrong turn in the dark.

it is beautiful in the way storms are beautiful, and frightening in the way cathedrals are frightening: too much life stacked in one place, too much silence between the living and the almost-living. cities throng. markets shout. forges ring. lovers steal hours under towers that will still be standing when the hills around them have moved again.

peoples share this ground in uneasy company — humans stitching law through the chaos, orcs hammering steel hotter than human forges, elves arguing green line against ice line — and others stranger still, each with a capital, a craft, and a hunger that is not the next door’s.

and over all of them hangs the long winter: the undead pressure at the rim of the world. not one army with a banner, but bones of every kind that refuse to lie down — a dry walking on the tundra, a memory that will not finish dying. the living hold the center. the rim keeps pushing back.

always a new horizon. always a new deal in a tent that may not be there tomorrow. always a treasure someone swears is worth the risk — gold, a name, a map true for one more night. fortune favors the bold. the land favors no one.
